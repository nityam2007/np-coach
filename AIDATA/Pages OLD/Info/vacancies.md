# Vacancies

Feel free to contact us using the form below to see if we have any vacancies. We will aim to answer within 48 hours.

## DRIVER JOBS

- As a driver with New Punjab Coaches you will have a passion to provide exceptional customer service.
- With an inclusive attitude you will make a big difference to the people and communities we serve.
- Whether you are operating our school bus services, private hire, airport transfers, or Day tours.
- We have driving opportunities with a variety of work patterns that reflect the services that we provide.
- We are looking for safe, professional, friendly drivers to drive our buses and coaches, enjoy our occasional staff lunches, evenings out and even holidays away.
- We will be delighted to support you in every aspect to becoming a professional New Punjab Coaches driver.

## What you'll need

- A valid UK PCV licence (Category D)
- A passion to deliver great customer service
- Aged 21 or over
- The ability to load and unload passenger luggage

### What we offer in return for your hard work and commitment

- A competitive Hourly rate
- Guaranteed 45 hours paid per week, with the ability to work overtime
- A free smart comfortable uniform
- Paid annual leave
- No washing/refilling/cleaning of vehicle when back to yard.

## Things to Note

We are really proud of our health and safety record and as a result, we operate a Drugs and Alcohol Policy which is applicable to all employees.

As part of your initial assessment, You may be subject to random tests during your employment.
