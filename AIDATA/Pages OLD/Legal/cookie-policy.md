# Cookie Policy

## Overview

NP Coaches uses cookies to enhance services and analyze website usage. The policy was last updated on 08/06/2023.

## Purpose of Cookies

Cookies are small text files downloaded to devices when visiting the website. They enable the site to recognize devices and improve user experience.

The company uses cookies for several purposes:

- **Traffic monitoring**: Software tracks customer patterns and usage to develop website design and layout, recognizing IP addresses without collecting personal data
- **Communication preferences**: Respects user choices, such as declining the live chat function
- **Email and survey tracking**: Third parties use cookies to understand how customers interact with communications and content
- **Session management**: Establishes and maintains user identity for services like the My Tour Manager self-service portal

## Managing Cookies

Users can delete cookies through their browser, though some features may be limited without them. Most website features remain accessible without cookies.

### How to Change Cookie Settings

Web browsers offer cookie control options. For detailed guidance on managing cookies across different browsers, users can visit:

- www.aboutcookies.org
- www.allaboutcookies.org

The company notes plans to enhance its cookie tool to allow users easier control over cookie settings after initial selection.
