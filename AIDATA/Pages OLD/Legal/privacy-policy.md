# Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions - NP Coaches

## Privacy Policy

**Last Update: 25/09/2021**

### Introduction

New Punjab Coaches respects user privacy. This policy explains how the company collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when visitors access www.gamechique.com and related platforms.

The company reserves rights to modify this policy at any time, with changes effective immediately upon posting. Continued site use indicates acceptance of revised terms.

### Information Collection

The company may gather various data types:

- **Personal Data**: Names, addresses, email, phone numbers, demographics (age, gender, hometown, interests)
- **Derivative Data**: IP addresses, browser type, operating system, access times, browsing history
- **Financial Data**: Payment method details stored by payment processor Stripe
- **Social Media Data**: Information from Facebook, Google+, Instagram, Pinterest, Twitter and similar platforms
- **Mobile Device Data**: Device IDs, models, manufacturers, location information
- **Contest/Survey Data**: Information from promotional participation
- **Mobile Application Data**: Geo-location, device access permissions, push notifications

### Information Use

The company uses collected information to:

- Administer contests and promotions
- Assist law enforcement and respond to subpoenas
- Create and manage accounts
- Deliver targeted advertising and newsletters
- Process payments and refunds
- Prevent fraud and criminal activity
- Monitor usage and improve services
- Resolve disputes and troubleshoot problems

### Information Disclosure

Information may be shared with:

- Third-party service providers (payment processors, email delivery, hosting)
- Marketing partners (with consent)
- Affiliates and business partners
- Third-party advertisers
- Other users (through interactions and public posts)
- Successor entities in case of merger, acquisition, or bankruptcy

### Tracking Technologies

- **Cookies and Web Beacons**: Used to customize the site; users can typically reject cookies via browser settings
- **Internet-Based Advertising**: Third-party software manages ads and marketing; users can opt-out via Network Advertising Initiative or Digital Advertising Alliance tools
- **Website Analytics**: Google Analytics and similar vendors track user behavior

### Account Management

Users may review, update, or terminate accounts by logging into account settings or contacting the company. Some information may be retained for fraud prevention and legal compliance.

### GDPR Compliance

The company follows eight GDPR principles including rights to information, access, rectification, erasure, processing restrictions, data portability, and objection.

## Home to School Transport Terms & Conditions

**Last Updated: 10/07/2024**

### 1. Payment Information

- Fares cover stated school year duration (daily, AM, PM, or yearly options)
- Payment required in advance
- Direct debit payments collected by GoCardless: initial deposit plus eight monthly installments (September-April)
- Failed direct debit surcharge: £20
- Direct debit cancellation immediately voids passes with no refund consideration
- Credit reference agencies may verify address and credit status
- Two-week processing time for initial direct debit applications

**Fare Review & Operating Cost Adjustment**: The company reserves rights to adjust fares during academic year due to fuel, insurance, wages, or regulatory cost increases. Parents receive advance notice; those declining revised prices may cancel before implementation. Pro-rata refunds apply for annual prepayments on remaining unused weeks.

### 2. Cancellation/Refunds

- Service cancellation occurs if economically unviable (insufficient sales or extreme cost increases)
- Pre-September cancellations receive full refunds; mid-year cancellations receive pro-rata refunds based on 38-week school years
- Cancellation notice must be submitted during preceding term via email to info@np-coaches.co.uk
- Once purchased, passes are non-refundable
- No refunds for student school exclusion or service exclusion
- No refunds if government or school authorities close schools
- No refunds during COVID-19, pandemics, fires, or similar closures
- Direct debit cancellation without notification immediately voids passes

### 3. Boarding/Pass E-Ticket

- E-tickets available immediately in ShuttleID customer portal after payment
- Children must show boarding passes to drivers at every journey ("NO TICKET – NO RIDE")
- Company reserves rights to withdraw e-tickets if false information provided or payment not received
- No allowance for absences (sickness, holidays, school outings, work experience, exclusion)
- £20 admin charge applies for service transfers

### 4. Bus Travel Etiquette

Expectations include:

- Reasonable behavior without driver distraction
- Remaining seated and using seatbelts where fitted
- No food or drink consumption
- No object throwing or abusive language

**Disciplinary Process**:
- Strike 1: Verbal warning
- Strike 2: Written warning (letter, SMS, or email) plus 7-day ban
- Strike 3: Pass voided; new passes available at full yearly cost (discretionary)

Immediate exclusion results from bus damage, arson, or violent conduct with recovery action for repair and replacement costs.

### 5. Service Information

- Service operates throughout academic year following school timetable
- Bus failures trigger alternative vehicle provision
- Notifications sent via SMS for delays
- Passholders should arrive 5 minutes before scheduled pickup

### 6. Service Alteration

The company reserves rights to amend timetables or routes with minimum two-hour notice, or transfer passengers to alternative services with minimum two-week notice. Refunds calculated per section 2.

### 7. Guests

Bona-fide passholder guests may travel occasionally, subject to seat availability, at £15.00 per person per day journey pass (at driver discretion).

### 8. Safeguarding/Safety

The company may suspend services for safeguarding or safety concerns and is not liable for alternative transportation in such cases.

### 9. Public Disparagement

The company may void passes without refund if children, parents, or guardians publicly defame the company through social media, online reviews, or group chats without first submitting formal written complaints to Info@np-coaches.co.uk. Voided passes proceed without prior notification. Appeals may be submitted via email.

### 10. Notifications & Tracking

Tracking and notifications are free extras not forming part of the service contract. The company holds no responsibility if these services become inoperative and reserves rights to withdraw them at any time.

### 11. GDPR

Privacy policies are accessible at https://www.np-coaches.co.uk/privacy-policy/. The company consults with schools regarding bullying, vehicle damage, and incidents. CCTV footage, where available, may be shared with schools and enforcement authorities as required.
