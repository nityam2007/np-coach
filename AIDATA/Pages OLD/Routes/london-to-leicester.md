The London to Leicester Express Service now runs "Friday, Saturday, Sunday & Monday" with no service Tuesday through Thursday.

## Key Travel Requirements

Passengers must book online tickets at least one hour before departure. The company allows "1 suitcase (20kg) and 1 Hand Luggage (7kg)" per ticket, with surcharges for additional luggage. "No Refunds are permitted on any ticket sales," and all travelers must arrive 15 minutes early.
