F.A.Q
-----

[Does the child need to carry the ticket every day Morning and after school?](#collapse-70c80156a36be9948273)

Yes, it’s a QR code they must scan onto the bus with, We have a strict policy of no ticket no ride. Under no circumstances will a child be able to ride without a valid/scannable QR Code.

[How will the child know which bus to take after school in the evening/afternoon?](#collapse-1949a2a6a36be9948273)

Bus service number/codes are very clearly displayed, if unsure please ask anyone from the transport team.

[Does the bus stop get announced or does someone announce the child's name for the bus stop the kid needs to step out?](#collapse-77932636a36be9948273)

Every child is responsible for getting off at the correct bus stop. Our published timetable clearly shows which order the bus drops off.

[How can I track the bus and see if my child is on the bus?](#collapse-61d187e6a36be9948273)

Please go to [https://passenger.shuttleid.uk/](https://passenger.shuttleid.uk/)  > Log in > select notifications >  Enable the button for EMAIL BOARDING NOTIFICATIONS

[My child forgot his QR code, can he scan on with his friends code?](#collapse-933885f6a36be9948273)

If any child is found sharing a QR code, we hold the right to cancel the QR code, which means you will have to repurchase the full year ticket again to be able to travel on the services.

[The bus goes past my house, can you just stop and drop my child off?](#collapse-da128856a36be9948273)

We would love to provide everyone with a door to door service, no, we are only permitted stop at pre- authorised bus stops we hold permits for.

[I pulled up in my car and the bus drove off, why?](#collapse-ad7307c6a36be9948273)

All buses leave on time whenever possible, we will never leave early, so please ensure you are all at your stops 3 minutes before scheduled time.

[I need to change my bank details for the direct debit, how do I do that?](#collapse-baf78996a36be9948273)

You can contact **GoCardless** directly by calling [**020 7183 8674**](tel:02071838674) to update your bank details.

[Why is it so important to have a pass, the driver knows my child?](#collapse-10b6c806a36be9948273)

Is it important and a legal obligation for all passengers to be Manifested. scanning the pass enables us to trace the child and their guardian/ parent in case of an emergency, we will know who to contact. Without a child scanning onboard, we have no trace.

[My child lost his phone/doesn’t have a phone. how can they scan on?](#collapse-5bc4d1e6a36be9948273)

Have a printed paper copy of your pass to ensure your child is always covered. We reject passengers without correct passes.

#### **Rules :**

*   Do not pickup or drop children at the bus stop, Its illegal and can get us suspended from using that bus stop. If our drivers see this happening, we will suspend your child’s travel for up to 5 days.
*   Always Park with respect for other road users and residents.
*   Do not block the bus with your vehicle, we will suspend your child’s travel for up to 5 days.
