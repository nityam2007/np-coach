Lost Property
-------------

Think you have lost some property on one of our coaches? Feel free to fill in our form below and we will let you know.

There will be a £5 service charge on collection of any item/s which are re-claimed. This is to cover administration and storage cost (excluding VAT). _Items must be reclaimed from the depot within 7 days of travel to prevent additional storage costs. Lost property is only held for a maximum of 14 days after which it is destroyed._
