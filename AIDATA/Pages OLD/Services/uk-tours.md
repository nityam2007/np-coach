## Locations

New Punjab Coaches Ltd has an extensive list of destinations, we are able to meet all your needs and even secure group discounts for entrance to major attractions. Our locations include:

### London

Take in a tour of our capital city and visit some of London’s historical buildings. Watch the changing of the guard at Buckingham Palace or spend some time in one of the many wonderful art galleries and museums.


[Get a Quote](https://np-coaches.co.uk/get-a-quote/)

### Windsor

A short journey out of London will take you to the Queen’s favourite residence, Windsor Castle, an estate that dates back to the 12th century. You’ll have the chance to tour the castle and surrounding areas, including a cruise on the river Thames.


[Get a Quote](https://np-coaches.co.uk/get-a-quote/)

### Hampton Court

Located in Surrey, Hampton Court is the famous Tudor palace of King Henry VIII. Explore the palace’s many historic attractions, from the great hall and kitchens to the world-renowned gardens.


[Get a Quote](https://np-coaches.co.uk/get-a-quote/)

### Cornwall

Visit Cornwall in the south-west of the country and discover the area’s close association with King Arthur. This includes Tintagel, Cadbury Castle, and Glastonbury, where Arthur is said to have been buried.


[Get a Quote](https://np-coaches.co.uk/get-a-quote/)

### Salisbury

Venture further afield and visit the city of Salisbury with its magnificent cathedral, which is home to the tallest spire in the country at a staggering 404 feet. Journey to the Stonehenge monument, a mysterious wonder of the world that dates back to 3,500 BC.

[Get a Quote](https://np-coaches.co.uk/get-a-quote/)

### John Wesley Methodist Church

Visit some of the historic places associated with John Wesley. This includes Epworth, where John’s father was rector, and Bristol, where he preached his first open-air sermon and founded the first Methodist chapel

[Get a Quote](https://np-coaches.co.uk/get-a-quote/)

### Bath

Bath is a beautiful Georgian city, where you’ll find the famous spa and Roman baths. The home of the Bath Bun, Sally Lun’s restaurant, which dates back to the 15th century. You’ll also see the Bath Abbey, known as the lantern of the west.

[Get a Quote](https://np-coaches.co.uk/get-a-quote/)
